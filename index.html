✨ What Is Clockify?

Clockify is a time tracking service built for the web. It lets people record how many hours they spend on tasks, projects and clients, and then turns that information into clear reports and timesheets. It is used by small businesses, large teams, agencies and solo freelancers who need to know where their working hours actually go.

Instead of guessing how long a job took, you press a start button when you begin and a stop button when you finish. The time is saved automatically and organised by project. At the end of the week, you have a complete record of your work hours — ready to share with a client, a manager or an accountant.

Because it is a web-based application, there is nothing heavy to maintain. You open it, you track your time, and you close it. That simplicity is exactly why so many teams rely on it every single day.
